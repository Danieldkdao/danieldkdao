- 🧠 Building **BrainQuest**, a cross-platform platform for strengthening critical-thinking and problem-solving skills

## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 🧠 [BrainQuest](https://github.com/Danieldkdao/brain-quest)

A cross-platform cognitive training and learning platform designed to make critical thinking engaging and rewarding. It includes personalized exercises, daily plans, progress tracking, XP, streaks, educational content, community features, classrooms, and assignments.

`Next.js` `React Native` `Expo` `TypeScript` `AI SDK` `Drizzle ORM`

---

### ⚡ [ProdoDesk](https://github.com/Danieldkdao/prododesk)

An AI-powered personal productivity workspace for organizing tasks, projects, documents, priorities, and focus. It combines a calendar-based task system with rich documents and an AI agent capable of understanding and acting on workspace data.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Neon` `Drizzle ORM` `AI SDK` `Tiptap`

---

### ⚔️ [Synapse](https://github.com/Danieldkdao/synapse)

A competitive programming platform where developers can participate in real-time coding battles, watch matches, connect with other developers, and practice with an integrated AI coach called the Oracle.

[Live Demo](https://synapsedev.up.railway.app) · `Next.js` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `AI SDK` `Real-time Systems`

---

### ➗ [CollabMath](https://github.com/Danieldkdao/collab-math)

A collaborative platform for discussing mathematical problems through organized threads, comments, Markdown, and LaTeX. It was created to help support real mathematical collaboration.

[Live Demo](https://collabmath.vercel.app) · `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Neon` `Drizzle ORM` `KaTeX`

---

### 📚 [Learn Macaulay2](https://github.com/Danieldkdao/M2website)

A prototype redesign of the Macaulay2 learning and documentation experience. I built and presented it while attending a Macaulay2 workshop in Atlanta, with a focus on making the system more approachable for new users.

`Macaulay2` `Documentation` `Education` `Web Design`

---

### 🎾 [Lawrence Rally Partners](https://github.com/Danieldkdao/lawrence-rally-partners)

A production website for my tennis and pickleball practice-partner business. It helps players discover our service, submit bookings, and receive session confirmations.

[Live Demo](https://lawrencerallypartners.vercel.app) · `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Drizzle ORM` `GSAP`
